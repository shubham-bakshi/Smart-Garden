# LetPlant

**Category:** AI plant-care app plus proprietary hardware. **Emerging direct competitor.**
**Physical sensor:** **SDESA**, described as a patent-pending environmental-intelligence system. Rolling out during 2026; price, availability and shipping are unverified.

## Features

- **Plant ID** (plant.id): company claims 500,000+ species and 98% accuracy under stated conditions. These are not independently validated.
- Care guide, health diagnosis, pet-toxicity info, difficulty charts, growth calendar, plant history.
- **SDESA** monitors soil moisture, light, temperature, humidity/air conditions and air quality.
- **Proactive alerts** before visible stress appears.
- The core app is free in India (identification, toxicity, diagnostics).

## Why it matters

- It is very close to our original concept, and it moves from calendar reminders ("water every 7 days") to state-based monitoring ("soil is drying faster than expected").
- Do **not** use it as a hardware cost benchmark until SDESA price, availability and shipping are verified.
- Conductivity, watering-event detection, room-level sensing and sensor-failure fallback are all "not established".
