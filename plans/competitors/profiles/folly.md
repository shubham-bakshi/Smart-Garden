# Folly

**Category:** Software-first adaptive plant care ("care that adapts to your setup, season and weather").
**Physical sensor:** No dedicated sensor evidence found.

## Features

- Plant identification and **Plant Doctor** (photo → visible symptoms → likely causes → treatment plan).
- **Smart Care:** watering adapts to room, pot, light, season and local weather.
- **Weather alerts** per plant (heat, frost, storms).
- Seasonal care guides, plant collection, **multiple growing sites** (e.g. Balcony vs Living Room), reminders.

## Takeaways for us

- Shows that plant identity + environment + season + weather → adaptive recommendations works without hardware. Reinforces that personalization is not our differentiator.
- Multi-site microclimates are worth borrowing (maps to our room-level sensing idea).
- Covers broader plant health, not just watering.
