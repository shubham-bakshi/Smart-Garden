Conduct a detailed market research study of **ready-to-use smart plant/soil sensors available for purchase in India**.

### Objective

I am exploring a smart indoor-plant management product consisting of:

- Multiple low-cost sensors placed in individual plant pots
- A central hub/gateway that collects data from all sensors
- A mobile app that receives the data from the hub
- The long-term goal is to provide intelligent plant-care recommendations

I am specifically interested in understanding the **existing hardware market** and identifying whether there is an opportunity to build a significantly cheaper solution.

### Important scope

Focus ONLY on **finished, ready-to-use consumer/prosumer sensors** that I can buy and start using immediately.

Do NOT include:
- Bare soil-moisture probes
- Arduino modules
- ESP32 development boards
- Individual electronic components
- DIY sensor circuits
- Sensors that require me to build the electronics myself

You MAY include products that require a compatible hub/gateway, because that is directly relevant to our proposed architecture.
